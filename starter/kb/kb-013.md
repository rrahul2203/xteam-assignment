# Network and Transfer Charges

Transfers out of your account are charged at 0.4% of the transfer amount, plus
the network cost quoted at the time you confirm. There is no minimum charge.
The 0.4% platform component and the network component are itemised separately
on the confirmation screen so you can see what is paid to us and what is paid
to the network. Outbound fiat transfers to a linked bank account are charged
$1.50. If a transfer fails, both components are returned to your balance
automatically within one business day.
