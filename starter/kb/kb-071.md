# Debit Card Purchase Fees

Buying crypto with a debit card carries a 3.99% processing fee, applied on top
of the quoted spread. The debit card purchase fee covers the cost charged by
the card networks and is not refundable once the trade has settled. Card
purchases are credited instantly. Some issuing banks treat crypto card
purchases as a cash advance and may apply their own additional charge, which
we do not control and cannot refund.
