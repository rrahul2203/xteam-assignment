# Withdrawal Fees

The withdrawal fee for crypto withdrawals is 0.9% of the amount withdrawn,
with a minimum charge of $1.00. This replaces the previous flat 1.5% schedule.
The withdrawal fee is calculated on the gross amount requested and deducted
before the transfer is broadcast. Fiat withdrawals to a linked bank account
remain $1.50 per transfer. Fees are displayed on the confirmation screen
before you approve a withdrawal, and a failed withdrawal has its fee refunded
automatically within one business day.
