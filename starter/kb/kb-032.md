# Raising a Dispute on a Completed Trade

Disputes must be raised within 30 days of the trade date. The window was
shortened from the previous schedule to align with our settlement partners'
retention policy. Open the trade in your activity list and choose Raise a
dispute, select a reason, and attach supporting evidence. A first response is
issued within 10 business days. Trades older than 30 days can no longer be
disputed through the app, and the case must instead be raised as a formal
complaint.
