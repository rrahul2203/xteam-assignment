# Staking Rewards Rates

Current staking reward rates are 4.2% APY for ETH, 5.6% APY for SOL and 3.0%
APY for ADA. Staking rewards accrue daily and are paid to your balance every
Monday. Assets that are staked remain subject to an unbonding period set by
the underlying network, during which they cannot be sold or transferred.
Reward rates are variable and are reviewed quarterly. The rate shown on the
staking screen at the moment you stake is not locked in for the duration of
the stake.
