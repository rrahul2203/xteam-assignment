# Regional Availability

The service is available in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area,
Brazil, India, Canada and Australia. Product availability differs by region,
and staking, card funding and recurring buys are not offered everywhere. The
regional entity that serves you is shown at the bottom of your account
settings page. You must use the entity for the country in which you are
resident for tax purposes.
