# Tax Documents and Statements

Annual tax documents for the previous calendar year are published by February
15 and can be downloaded from the Reports section of your account. Statements
are available as CSV and PDF. If you traded on more than one regional entity
you will see one document per entity. We do not provide tax advice and the
documents are informational only. Corrections issued after publication are
versioned, and the Reports section always lists the most recent version first.
