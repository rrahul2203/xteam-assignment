# Earn Rates for Supported Assets

Earn rates are currently 3.1% APY for ETH, 4.4% APY for SOL and 2.2% APY for
ADA. Rewards accrue daily and settle to your balance every Monday. Staked
assets are subject to the unbonding period of the underlying network and
cannot be sold or transferred while unbonding. Rates are variable, are
reviewed quarterly, and the rate displayed when you stake is not fixed for the
life of the position.
