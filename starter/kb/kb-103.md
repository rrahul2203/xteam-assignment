# Order Types Explained

A market order executes immediately at the best price currently available, so
the amount you receive can differ from the amount quoted when you tapped buy.
A limit order executes only at your specified price or better, and rests
unfilled until the market reaches it or you cancel it. Limit orders can be
partially filled. Stop orders trigger a market order once a trigger price is
reached and carry the same price uncertainty as any market order.
