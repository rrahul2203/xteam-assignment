# Recurring Buys

Recurring buys can be scheduled daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly. The
order executes at the market price at the scheduled time. You may pause or
cancel a recurring buy at any time from the Recurring section, and a
cancellation takes effect immediately for any execution that has not already
started. If a scheduled execution fails because of insufficient funds, it is
skipped and retried at the next interval.
