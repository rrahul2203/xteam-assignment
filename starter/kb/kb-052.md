# Verification Tiers and Transfer Ceilings

Transfer ceilings are set by verification tier. Tier 1 is capped at $5,000 per
day, Tier 2 at $50,000 per day and Tier 3 at $250,000 per day. Ceilings reset
at 00:00 UTC and apply jointly across purchases, sales and outbound transfers.
Moving to a higher tier requires proof of address and a source of funds
declaration, and is usually reviewed within two business days. Institutional
ceilings are agreed separately and are not covered by this schedule.
