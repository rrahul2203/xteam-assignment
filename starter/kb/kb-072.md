# Card Funding Charges

Funding a purchase with a linked card is charged at 2.49% of the purchase
value, applied on top of the quoted spread. Card funded orders are credited
instantly. The charge is not refundable once a trade has settled. Your card
issuer may separately classify the payment as a cash advance and apply its own
charge, which is outside our control and cannot be refunded by us.
