# Reporting Fraud

To report fraud, email our fraud team with your account email, the transaction
identifiers involved and a short description of what happened. A fraud analyst
will acknowledge your report and respond within 5 business days. Do not share
your password, recovery phrase or one time codes with anyone, including anyone
claiming to be from support. If you believe your credentials have been
exposed, change your password immediately and revoke any active sessions from
the security settings page.
