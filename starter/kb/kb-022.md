# Restoring Access to Your Account

SMS based recovery has been discontinued and is no longer offered for any
account. To restore access when you no longer hold your enrolled device,
submit an identity verification request containing a government issued photo
ID and a short video selfie. Verification decisions are issued within 72
hours. Withdrawals remain disabled for 72 hours after a successful restore, as
a precaution against account takeover. If the verification is rejected you may
resubmit once every 30 days.
