# Reporting Unauthorized Activity

Unauthorized activity must be reported from the in-app Report activity flow,
which is monitored continuously. Filing a report places an automatic 24 hour
freeze on outbound transfers while the case is triaged. A specialist responds
within 1 business day. Email reports are no longer monitored for new cases.
Never share your password, recovery phrase or one time codes with anyone,
including anyone presenting themselves as support staff. Revoke active
sessions from the security settings page as soon as you suspect exposure.
