# Scheduled Maintenance Notice

Withdrawals and outbound transfers are paused for scheduled maintenance from
2026-06-12 22:00 UTC until 2026-06-14 06:00 UTC. During this window you can
still buy, sell and hold, but any withdrawal you submit will be queued and
released once maintenance completes. Deposits are unaffected. We expect no
data loss and no action is required from you. Queued withdrawals are processed
in submission order once the window closes.
