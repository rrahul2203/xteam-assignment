# Daily Account Limits

Daily account limits depend on your verification tier. Tier 1 accounts may
transact up to $2,500 per day. Tier 2 accounts may transact up to $25,000 per
day. Tier 3 accounts may transact up to $100,000 per day. Limits reset at
00:00 UTC. Raising your daily limit requires completing the next verification
tier, which typically means supplying proof of address and a source of funds
declaration. Limits apply jointly to purchases, sales and outbound transfers.
