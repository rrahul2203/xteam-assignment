# Funding Your Account by Bank Transfer

Bank transfers under $10,000 now clear the same day when they are received
before 16:00 UTC on a business day. Transfers at or above $10,000, and any
transfer received after the cut off, clear within 1 business day. Funds show
as pending until cleared and cannot be traded or withdrawn before then.
Transfers that fail bank verification are returned to the sending account,
which may add up to 3 business days.
