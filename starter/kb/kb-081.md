# Bank Deposit Processing Times

Bank deposits take 3-5 business days to clear before the funds become
tradable. The deposit will appear in your account as pending as soon as we are
notified by the sending bank, but it cannot be traded or withdrawn until it
has fully cleared. Deposits initiated on a weekend or public holiday begin
processing on the next business day. Deposits that fail bank verification are
returned to the sending account, which can add a further 3 business days.
