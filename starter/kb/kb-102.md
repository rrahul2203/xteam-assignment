# Supported Assets

The following assets are supported for buying, selling, holding and transfer:
BTC, ETH, USDC, USDT, SOL, ADA, XRP, LTC, DOGE and MATIC. Asset availability
varies by region and some assets are unavailable in certain jurisdictions for
regulatory reasons. Assets not on this list cannot be deposited, and any
deposit of an unsupported asset may be unrecoverable. New listings are
announced in the product changelog before they become tradable.
