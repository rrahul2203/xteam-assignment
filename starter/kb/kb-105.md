# Price Alerts

Price alerts are configured per asset from the asset detail screen. You may
set up to 20 active alerts per account. Alerts are delivered as push
notifications and require notifications to be enabled at the operating system
level. An alert fires once and then becomes inactive until you re-enable it.
Alerts are evaluated against our reference price, which can differ slightly
from the last traded price on any single venue.
