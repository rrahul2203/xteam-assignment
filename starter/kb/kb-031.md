# Transaction Dispute Window

You may dispute a transaction within 60 days of the transaction date. Disputes
raised after the 60 day window cannot be investigated because the underlying
settlement records are no longer retained by our payment partners. To dispute
a transaction, open the transaction in your history and choose Dispute this
transaction. You will be asked to select a reason and provide any supporting
evidence. Most disputes receive a first response within 10 business days.
