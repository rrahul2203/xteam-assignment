# Withdrawal Fees

A flat withdrawal fee of 1.5% applies to all crypto withdrawals from your
account, with a minimum charge of $2.00 per withdrawal. The withdrawal fee is
calculated on the gross amount you request and is deducted before the transfer
is broadcast to the network. Withdrawal fees are shown on the confirmation
screen before you approve the withdrawal. Fiat withdrawals to a linked bank
account are charged separately at $1.50 per transfer. If a withdrawal fails
after the fee has been deducted, the fee is automatically refunded to your
balance within one business day.
