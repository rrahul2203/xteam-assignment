# Referral Bonus Program

Invite a friend and you both receive a $25 bonus once they complete identity
verification and their first trade of at least $100. The referral bonus is
credited within 7 days of the qualifying trade. There is no limit on the
number of friends you may refer, but bonuses are capped at $500 per account
per calendar year. The program runs until 2026-03-31 and referrals completed
after that date do not qualify.
