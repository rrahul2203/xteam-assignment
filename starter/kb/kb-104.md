# Moving Crypto to an External Wallet

To send crypto to an external wallet, add the destination address to your
address whitelist first. Newly whitelisted addresses are subject to a 24 hour
hold before they can receive a transfer, which limits the damage an attacker
can do with a stolen session. Always confirm the network as well as the
address, since sending on the wrong network usually makes funds unrecoverable.
Transfers cannot be reversed once broadcast to the network.
