# Closing Your Account

Before an account can be closed, all balances must be withdrawn or converted,
and any open orders cancelled. Request closure from the account settings page.
Closure is processed within 5 business days. Certain records are retained
after closure where we are legally required to keep them, and a closed account
cannot be reopened, so a new application is required if you return later.
