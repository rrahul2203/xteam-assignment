# Service Degradation Notice

We are aware that card funded purchases are completing more slowly than usual.
Affected orders may take up to 30 minutes to appear in your balance instead of
the usual few seconds. The order is not lost and no duplicate charge is
applied, so please do not resubmit the purchase. Bank transfers, withdrawals
and trading are unaffected. This notice remains in force until further notice
while the fix is rolled out.
